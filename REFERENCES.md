# References

The bibliography of the series, grouped by post. A source cited by several posts is listed once, under the post it primarily belongs to (its topical home), and the other posts point back to that entry, so a source may be cited in an earlier post than the one it is filed under. URLs are given where a stable canonical URL exists; living vendor documentation is cited at its documentation page, with the years it was read. Sources shared with the `context-engineering` series are listed here in full as well, and a post of that series is named by its id (`ctx-005`).

Citation style: author or organisation, title in quotation marks, venue or publisher in italics, year, then the URL, and where the point a source supports is not obvious, a short note after a colon. A **bold** bullet is a master entry, the full citation; a plain bullet is a cross-reference to a master entry filed under another post (`Surname, "Title": see post NN`), so nothing is listed twice.

Two works by one author under two mastheads count as one source: Osmani's "Agent Harness Engineering" (personal blog and *O'Reilly Radar*) is a single entry, as is his "Loop engineering". Where a publisher has two genuinely different articles (MindStudio's harness explainer and its Stripe throughput piece), they are two entries, and each says so.

## Post 04 - Why the harness beats the model: the evidence

- **Zhang, Y., Wang, J., Ge, Y., Xu, W., Hamm, J., and Reddy, C. K.** "Stop Comparing LLM Agents Without Disclosing the Harness." *arXiv:2605.23950*, 7 May 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23950: the Binding Constraint Thesis: for long-horizon tasks across models of comparable frontier capability, "performance variance is governed more by harness configuration than by model choice, and current evaluation protocols therefore systematically misattribute harness-level gains to model improvements". A control-theoretic formalisation of the harness as controller, evidence that harness-induced variance can exceed model-induced variance including model ranking reversal, and a disclosure standard with a variance-decomposition protocol.
- **Starace, J.** "Scaffold Effects on GAIA: A Controlled Comparison." *arXiv:2606.08529*, 7 June 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08529: a pre-registered controlled comparison of three scaffolds (ReAct, planner-actor-rater, planner-then-executor) across five models from three providers on GAIA validation Levels 1 and 2, tasks and conditions held fixed, three attempts per question. "Scaffold choice alone moves measured accuracy by as much as 28 percentage points within a single model (Opus, Level 2, robust slice)"; the term *elicitation gap* (which Starace credits to METR's time-horizon work, Kwa et al., 2026) and the finding that it "is not guaranteed to shrink as models improve".
- **Guo, J., et al.** "From Question Answering to Task Completion: A Survey on Agent System and Harness Design." *arXiv:2606.20683*, 14 June 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20683: the model-harness lens: whether the bottleneck sits in the foundation model, the execution harness, or the coupling between them; the harness decomposed into six runtime responsibilities (observation, context, control, action, state, verification).
- **Ding, F., Zhang, Y., Liu, R., Liao, Y., Zeng, Z., and Yang, H.** "Scaffold-Mediated Post-Training: Co-Evolving Model Parameters and Procedural Scaffold Graphs." *arXiv:2608.05156*, 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05156: the co-training flywheel run as an explicit training procedure (discovery, distillation, dynamic recompilation); on FeatureBench, discovered skills improve the passed rate by 8.1pp and the distilled model retains a 27.7% passed rate with no external scaffold, an 85.2% distillation retention rate. The evidence that harness gains are model-specific and do not transfer.
- **MindStudio.** "What Is Harness Engineering? ## Post 08 - State and the filesystem: durable memory and git as state

- **Aider.** "Git integration." *Aider documentation*, 2024 to 2026. https://aider.chat/docs/git.html: "whenever aider edits a file, it commits those changes with a descriptive commit message", written by the `--weak-model` from the diffs and the chat history; "`/undo` will undo and discard the last change". The checkpoint-and-rollback pattern shipping as a default. Distinct from the Aider entry in the `context-engineering` series' references, which cites the architect-mode documentation.
- **Anthropic.** "Rewind file changes with checkpointing." *Claude Agent SDK documentation*, 2025 to 2026. https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-sdk/file-checkpointing: backups taken before modifying files "through the Write, Edit, or NotebookEdit tools"; changes made through Bash commands are not captured; "file rewinding restores files on disk to a previous state.
